The wake-up call

Organizations want a lot from AI and have ambitious goals — and AI genuinely offers incredible possibilities. But it comes with a price. It doesn’t happen overnight; it takes a real transformation of the way we work.

Right now most people and organizations are still tiptoeing around it — postponing, uncertain, because there’s still too much unclear. That is exactly the impasse we started from. This is the wake-up call: the change isn’t on the horizon, it’s here. And BTM offers the clarity the impasse lacks — openness, one model, a shared vocabulary. Honestly, it’s not for everyone: it asks for discipline, focus and the right positive, professional attitude. That’s part of the provocation.
