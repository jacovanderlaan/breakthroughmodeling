2026-08-01Decision

A name bought for a shelf that outgrew itself

Counting the concept library: 269 entries, of which 19 are about building rather than modelling.

Model-driven data engineering is coding and modelling, and only the modelling half has ever had a front door. The theme was already sitting in the library under its own name — metadata as code, next to governance-as-code and policy-as-code — waiting for somewhere to live. So the name was registered and the site deliberately not built: nineteen concepts is a section, not a site. What makes the split cheap is that the two halves share everything except the shelf — the attitude material, the design base, the source of truth. Splitting by metadata rather than by folders means nothing has to move.

2026-07-24Session

Breakthrough Modeling, introduced at a major Dutch bank

Sparring session with the data team of one of the Netherlands' largest banks.

Not a pitch — a working session: put the modelling impasse on the table, question every assumption, model anew. The setting fit the idea: a curved, structural headquarters, and inside, a conversation about the structure you cannot see. What came out of it was a first request to turn the approach into something a team can run.
